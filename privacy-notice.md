# Privacy notice

Last updated: 10 November 2026

**The short version: we don't collect your data.** Everything you put into Cairnfold stays on your devices and in your family's own iCloud. We have no servers, no accounts, no analytics, no adverts and no tracking. We can't see your circle, even if we wanted to.

This notice explains exactly what Cairnfold stores, where, and who can see it.

## Who we are

Cairnfold is made by Faton Pantina ("we", "us"). Contact: aafdigital@proton.me.

Because we never receive the information you put in Cairnfold, **we are not a controller of it** under UK and EU data protection law. You and your family decide what goes in and who can see it. Apple stores it for you in iCloud under its own terms and privacy policy.

## What Cairnfold stores

Only what you and your family choose to add, for example:

- the person you care for: name, date of birth, NHS number, conditions, allergies, notes for paramedics and where their key papers are kept;
- your family: names, roles and colours;
- journal entries, appointments, tasks, rota shifts and time logged;
- medicine changes, exactly as written, with photos of boxes or letters if you add them;
- contacts such as the GP, pharmacy and neighbours.

Some of this is health information, which the law treats as especially sensitive. It stays private to your circle, and we never see it.

## Where it's kept

- **On your iPhone**, inside Cairnfold, protected by your device's encryption and, if you turn it on, by Face ID in Cairnfold.
- **In iCloud**, in the Lead's private iCloud database, and shared with the people the Lead invites through Apple's iCloud sharing. Each person joins with their own Apple Account.
  - Sensitive fields (medicines, conditions, allergies, notes, journal text and similar) are stored in iCloud's **encrypted fields**.
  - If you've turned on **Advanced Data Protection** for iCloud, Apple can't read them either.
- **Who in the circle sees what** depends on the level you choose for each item:
  - "Everyone in the circle";
  - "Siblings only", kept in a separate shared area that only Siblings can open;
  - "Lead only", kept in the Lead's iCloud and never shared.

## Things that stay on your device

- **Apple Intelligence and text recognition.** When Cairnfold reads a shared message or the label on a medicine box, it does so **on your iPhone**, using Apple's on-device models. Nothing is sent to us or to any AI service.
- **Face ID.** Handled entirely by iOS. Cairnfold only learns whether unlocking succeeded.
- **Photos, camera and contacts.**
  - Cairnfold only receives the photo you take or choose, and the one contact you pick. It never reads your photo library or address book.
  - Photos you add are shrunk and stored with the record, in your circle.
- **Sharing from WhatsApp, Messages or Mail.** The message you share is read on your iPhone. If you confirm it, the item is passed to Cairnfold through a private folder on your iPhone, then saved to your circle. Nothing joins your family chat.
- **The Lock Screen widget** is off until you turn it on. If you do, the details you choose (the person's name, and optionally allergies, conditions and who to call) are kept on this iPhone so they can be shown **without unlocking** it, for paramedics. Turn it off and they're removed.
- **Siri and Shortcuts.** When you say "Log a visit in Cairnfold", the entry is created on your iPhone. Siri's own processing of what you say is covered by Apple's privacy policy.

## When information leaves your circle

Only when you send it, for example by:

- **sharing** the Weekly summary, a Crisis Card PDF or QR code, a care record PDF or a circle file through the share sheet;
- **adding an appointment to your own calendar.** Only the title, time and place are copied, never notes.

Once you've sent something, it's in the hands of whoever and wherever you sent it to. The Weekly summary only ever includes items marked "Everyone in the circle". Exports include everything **you** can see, so keep them safe.

## Purchases

Purchases and subscriptions are handled by Apple. Apple tells us about sales in aggregate, for example how many people bought Cairnfold Family in a country. We never receive your name, Apple Account or payment details.

## Analytics and crash reports

Cairnfold contains **no analytics, advertising or crash-reporting code** and sends nothing to us or to anyone else. If you've chosen in iPhone Settings → Privacy & Security → Analytics & Improvements to **share with app developers**, Apple may pass us anonymous crash reports and usage statistics. We can't identify you from them, and you can turn this off at any time.

On the App Store, Cairnfold's privacy label is **"Data Not Collected"**.

## Keeping and deleting your information

Your information stays until you or your family delete it.

- **Delete an item:** delete it in Cairnfold. It's removed from everyone's devices in the circle.
- **Leave a circle that was shared with you:** open Family and sharing → Manage invitations, or remove it in your iCloud settings. The circle is removed from your devices.
- **Delete a circle you started:** stop sharing and delete it. It's removed from everyone's devices. **The others lose access**, so hand it over first if they still need it.
- **Delete everything Cairnfold has stored in your iCloud:** iPhone Settings → [your name] → iCloud → Manage Account Storage → Cairnfold → Delete Data.
- **Deleting the app** removes Cairnfold's data from that iPhone but not from iCloud.

## Your rights

Because we don't hold your information, there's nothing for us to give you, correct or delete: it's all within your control in the app and in iCloud. For information Apple holds in your iCloud account, see apple.com/privacy, where you can download or delete your data. You can also complain to the Information Commissioner's Office (ico.org.uk) or your local data protection authority.

## Children

Cairnfold is designed for adults caring for a family member and isn't intended for children to use.

## Changes to this notice

If we change how Cairnfold handles information, we'll update this notice and the date at the top, and tell you in the app before any change takes effect. We will never start collecting your data without asking first.
