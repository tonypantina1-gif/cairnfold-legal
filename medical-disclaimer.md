# Not medical advice

Last updated: 10 November 2026

**Cairnfold helps your family keep track of care. It does not give medical advice, and it is not a substitute for a doctor, nurse, pharmacist or other health professional.**

## In an emergency

**Call 999** (or 112) in the UK, or your local emergency number. Don't wait for Cairnfold, a message or a family member.

For urgent help that isn't an emergency, call **NHS 111** or visit 111.nhs.uk.

## What Cairnfold does and doesn't do

Cairnfold **records and organises** what your family tells it. It does **not**:

- diagnose, treat, monitor or prevent any illness or condition;
- interpret symptoms, test results or what a professional has said;
- calculate, check or suggest doses, or warn about interactions;
- watch over the person you care for, or raise alerts if something is wrong.

Cairnfold is not a medical device.

## Medicines

The Medication Change Log records each medicine **exactly as the prescriber or label wrote it**, with who changed it and why. Cairnfold never works out, changes or checks a dose.

- Always follow the instructions from the prescriber, the label and the pharmacist, not the app.
- Check the record against the medicine box or prescription, especially after a hospital stay or a change.
- If anything is unclear, ask the GP or pharmacist.

## Suggestions from Apple Intelligence and text recognition

When Cairnfold reads a message or a medicine label, it **suggests** what to record. These suggestions are made on your iPhone and **can be wrong**: words can be misread, dates mistaken or details missed.

Cairnfold only fills in a medicine instruction when it appears word for word in the message or on the label, and nothing is saved until you check it and tap Add. **You're responsible for checking every suggestion before saving it.**

## The Crisis Card

The Crisis Card shows only what your family has entered. It's only as accurate and up to date as those records.

- Keep it current, especially allergies, medicines and who to call.
- If no allergies have been recorded, it says "None recorded". That doesn't mean there are none.
- Paramedics and hospital staff will make their own assessment. The card is there to help, not to replace it.
- If you show it on the Lock Screen, anyone holding the phone can read it.

## Decisions about care

Decisions about someone's health and care belong to them, as far as they're able to make them, together with their family, carers and health professionals. Information in Cairnfold, including what other family members have written, may be incomplete or out of date. Please check anything important with the people involved and with professionals.
