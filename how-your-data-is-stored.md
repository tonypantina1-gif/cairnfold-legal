# How your data is stored

**In your family's own iCloud. Not on our servers: we don't have any.**

## On your iPhone

Everything is kept inside Cairnfold on your iPhone, protected by your phone's passcode. You can also lock Cairnfold with Face ID in Settings.

## In iCloud

Your circle is kept in the iCloud account of the person who started it, the Lead. It's shared with the people they invite, each using their own Apple Account. Changes appear on everyone's phones within moments.

Medicines, conditions, allergies, notes and journal entries are stored in iCloud's encrypted fields. With Advanced Data Protection turned on in iCloud, not even Apple can read them.

## Who sees what

- **Everyone in the circle:** everyone you've invited.
- **Siblings only:** kept in a separate shared space that only brothers and sisters can open.
- **Lead only:** kept in the Lead's iCloud, never shared.

## What we see

Nothing. No accounts, no analytics, no adverts, no tracking. When Cairnfold reads a message or a medicine label, it does so on your iPhone.

## What leaves your circle

Only what you choose to send, such as the Weekly summary, a Crisis Card or an export, and only to where you send it.

## If the Lead steps back

The circle lives in the Lead's iCloud. If they stop sharing or delete it, everyone else loses access. Use **Hand over the circle** first, and export a copy whenever you like.

## Taking it with you

Family and sharing → **Export everything** gives you a readable PDF and an open file, at any time, free.
